      Booking Policies

      💳 Payment

      After your booking is confirmed, you'll receive an invoice via email with payment instructions. We accept bank transfers, credit cards, and mobile payment methods. A 20% deposit is required to secure your booking, with the balance due 7 days before your adventure.

      📅 Cancellation

      Cancellations made 14 days or more before your booking date receive a full refund. Cancellations made 7–13 days before receive 50% refund. Cancellations within 6 days forfeit the entire booking. We offer free date rescheduling subject to availability.

      🚫 Weather Policy

      Your safety is our priority. In case of severe weather, we'll reschedule your adventure to the next available date at no extra cost. If a date doesn't work, we offer a full refund. Some activities may be adjusted based on conditions.

      👥 Group Bookings

      For groups of 8 or more, special rates and flexible payment terms may apply. Please contact us directly to discuss your group's needs and arrange custom pricing and scheduling.

      ⚠️ Fitness Requirements

      All activities require a basic level of fitness. We recommend reviewing activity details before booking. If you have concerns about physical requirements or mobility, please contact us—we're happy to discuss modifications or alternatives.

      📋 Important Info

      Participants must be able to follow safety instructions. Video documentation may occur during activities for promotional purposes (opt-out available). Insurance is recommended. By booking, you accept our terms and acknowledge the inherent risks of outdoor adventure activities.

      Have questions about pricing?

      If you have special requirements, need a custom group booking, or want to discuss your adventure in detail, we're here to help.
