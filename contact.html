Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking at least 2–3 weeks in advance to ensure availability. However, same-week bookings are sometimes possible—contact us to check.

What if I'm a complete beginner?

Perfect! All our activities are beginner-friendly. Our professional guides will provide full instruction and adapt the experience to your comfort level.

Are children welcome?

Yes! Most activities welcome children (age requirements vary). Kayaking accepts ages 6+, ziplining 8+, rock climbing 12+, and team challenges any age. Let us know if you have questions.

What happens if the weather is bad?

Safety comes first. We'll reschedule your adventure to the next available date free of charge. If rescheduling doesn't work, we offer a full refund.

Can we arrange a private guide?

Yes! Private group bookings can be arranged. Contact us with your group size and preferred activity, and we'll discuss custom pricing and scheduling.

What if I have dietary restrictions?

Let us know about dietary needs when booking. We'll do our best to accommodate allergies and preferences. Note that refreshments provided are limited, so you're welcome to bring your own.

Is photography included?

Our guides have cameras for activity documentation. You're welcome to bring your own camera or phone. Note that some water activities may limit photography. We share photos digitally after your adventure.

Do you offer refunds or rescheduling?

Absolutely. Cancellations 14+ days before your date get a full refund. Cancellations 7–13 days out get 50% back. Within 6 days, the booking is forfeited. Rescheduling is always free based on availability.
