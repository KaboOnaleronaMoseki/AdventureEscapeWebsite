Package Questions

Can I mix and match activities?

Absolutely! Use our fee calculator to build your own perfect combination. Mix kayaking, ziplining, rock climbing, and team challenges in any way you like.

Do discounts apply automatically?

Yes. When you add multiple activities to your booking, our system automatically calculates the tiered discount (5% for 2 activities, 10% for 3, and 15% for 4+).

Are group discounts available?

For groups of 8 or more people, special pricing and customization options may be available. Please contact us directly to discuss your group's specific needs.

Can we customize a package?

Yes! If none of our preset packages perfectly match your vision, our team can design a completely custom package tailored to your group's interests, fitness levels, and budget.

What if someone in our group can't do one of the activities?

No problem. We're flexible! Let us know during booking and we can adjust your package. Participants can pursue different activities on the same day if needed.

Ready to book a package?

Start by building your own combination on our fees calculator, or contact us to discuss a custom package for your group.
