Adventure with care

A little preparation helps everyone enjoy the outdoors with respect for the people around us and the places we visit.

Before you set out

Be ready for your experience

Requirements vary by activity and location. Follow your guide's current instructions and check your booking details before travelling.

Tell us what you need

Share relevant mobility, health, swimming-confidence, age, or dietary information with our team before booking. We can discuss activity requirements and whether an experience is suitable.

Follow the activity briefing

Listen to your guide, use the equipment as instructed, and ask questions whenever something is unclear. Participation requirements and equipment differ by activity.

Dress for the conditions

Wear practical clothing and footwear for your booked activity. Check the confirmation details for what to bring, including any water, sun protection, or spare layers recommended for the day.

Check weather and meeting details

Outdoor plans may change when conditions are unsuitable. Confirm your meeting point, arrival time, and the latest weather arrangements with the team before you leave.

Leave the outdoors better

Respect the places we explore

  • Follow your guide and stay on permitted routes.
  • Take your rubbish with you and avoid disturbing wildlife or plants.
  • Respect other visitors, local communities, and site access rules.
  • Use water and other resources thoughtfully during your visit.

Access rules can vary by location. Your guide will share the relevant guidance for your activity.

Ask our team

Have a question before booking?

Contact us about activity requirements, accessibility needs, or current conditions.
