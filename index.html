Who we are

Outdoor experiences that bring people together

Founded by Liam Daniels in 2024, Adventure Escape SA is an adventure tourism enterprise operating across the Western Cape, South Africa. We design professionally guided outdoor experiences that promote teamwork, fitness, and a genuine appreciation for nature.

Whether you are an individual chasing your next thrill, a family looking to unplug, or a company planning a team escape, our packages make it simple to get outside safely. Every adventure is sustainable, beginner-friendly, and backed by qualified guides.
