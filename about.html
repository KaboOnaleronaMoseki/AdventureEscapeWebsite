About Our Company

Discover the story, values, and team behind Adventure Escape SA's commitment to delivering world-class outdoor experiences in the Western Cape.

Our Story

From passion to profession

Adventure Escape SA was founded in 2024 by Liam Daniels, an experienced outdoor guide with a deep love for the Western Cape's natural landscapes. After years of guiding individuals and corporate groups through the region's most spectacular locations, Liam recognized an opportunity to create a professional, sustainable adventure tourism company that places safety, quality, and genuine connection with nature at its core.

Starting with a small team of hand-selected guides, we've built Adventure Escape SA on the principle that outdoor adventures should be accessible to everyone — whether you're a first-timer or a seasoned adventurer. Our growth has been intentional and measured, prioritizing guide quality and customer satisfaction over rapid expansion.

Today, Adventure Escape SA is a trusted name for guided outdoor experiences across the Western Cape, serving individuals, families, and corporate teams with professionalism and passion.

Why We Do This

Our mission and values

Our Mission

To provide professionally guided, eco-conscious outdoor adventures that inspire confidence, build community, and foster a lifelong appreciation for the natural beauty of the Western Cape.

Our Vision

To become the leading adventure tourism company in southern Africa, recognized for excellence in safety, environmental stewardship, and customer experience.

Safety First

Every decision we make prioritizes the well-being of our guests and guides. We invest continuously in training, equipment inspection, and risk management protocols.

Environmental Respect

We're committed to low-impact tourism practices that preserve the Western Cape's unique ecosystems, from its fynbos to its pristine coastlines.

Team Spirit

Whether you're booking solo or as a group, our activities are designed to build confidence, communication, and lasting memories together.

Inclusive Adventures

Adventure isn't just for the elite. We design experiences that welcome participants of varying abilities and backgrounds, from beginners to experts.
